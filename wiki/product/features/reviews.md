# Reviews

Where the CEO reviews a lead's branch as on a forge, and decides it (the CEO, 2026-10-21): the files it changed, their diff, comments pinned to lines or general, sent back to the lead as a request for changes, and the merge. Reviews are listed and decided **only here**, in each project's **Inbox** ([Project page](./project-page.md); the CEO, 2026-10-21: no review cards on the [Dashboard](./dashboard.md); 2026-10-31: no Reviews screen across projects, the Inbox per project instead); a repo's **Review** button in the project's [In flight](./project-pipeline.md#2-in-flight) opens its page. The model is [Review](../models/review.md); the flow around it, [Pipeline and merges](./pipeline-and-merges.md#review-and-merge).

## The Inbox

`/projects/<project>/inbox`, a section of the [Project page](./project-page.md), with the sidebar's count of what waits on the CEO there: the project's open requests, reviews included ([App shell](./app-shell.md#sidebar)). At most 920px wide, 32px between sections, no title (the sidebar names it).

Three sections, each under a [section title](../design/design-system.md#type) with its count (the CEO, 2026-11-02: titles that read as such): **Needs you**, always shown, the reviews to decide (`needs-review`, a panel of rows) then the project's other open requests (one [request panel](./project-pipeline.md#1-needs-you) each); then, each left out when empty, **Waiting on the lead** (`changes-requested`) and **Done** (merged, dismissed or withdrawn, the last 20). The server lists the reviews newest first (the latest opened).

- **Row** (grid `1fr auto`, padding `12px 16px`, the whole row a link to its page): the review's title (500, the lead's: [Review](../models/review.md)), then `<Lead> · <repos> · <branch>` (branch in Mono); on the right the status (`Needs your review`, `Changes requested`, `Merged`, `Dismissed`, `Withdrawn`) over its time.
- **Nothing needs you**: under its title, `Nothing needs you yet.` in the calm [empty style](../design/design-system.md#surfaces), whatever waits on the lead or is done.

## A review's page

`/projects/<project>/inbox/<id>`, inside the Inbox, with `?since=1` for the change since the last review.

- **Header** (padding `20px 24px 16px`): a square `←` button (32px, outline; back to the Inbox) left of the review's title (22px, on one line, cut with an ellipsis, the whole of it in its tooltip: the CEO, 2026-11-05), then `<Lead> · <branch> · <status> · <time> · card <card title>` (the branch links to its code in Repos, in the first repo of the review; the project is the one open; a subtle copy icon after it copies its name, a check showing it took: the CEO, 2026-11-02; the card links to its wiki entry). Under it the lead's body, when there is one, as a **one-line box** (the CEO, 2026-11-05: a long one pushed the diffs off the screen): its first line as plain words, cut, and `Description` on the right, the whole box a button; a click opens it whole in a popover (up to 720px wide, scrolling past 520px tall), its Markdown rendered as a request's body ([Request](../models/request.md)). A withdrawn review adds `Withdrawn: <reason>.`, then `Superseded by <review>.` (a link to it) when one holds all of it.
- **Which change** (a segmented control, only once the CEO requested changes on a repo still open): **Against its target** (the default: what a merge takes, `<target>...branch`, each repo against its own [target](./pipeline-and-merges.md#merge-targets)) or **Since your last review** (from the branch head at the last Request changes to its head now).
- **Layout**: the header on top; under it two panes, each scrolling on its own (the tree, and the diffs with the comments), so going to a file moves only the diffs and the tree stays where it was (the CEO, 2026-10-21); the decision bar at the foot.
- **File tree** (240px, on the left, a `--line` border to its right; hidden under 900px): one group per repo open in the review, `<repo> → <target>`, then its files (rows padded `6px 8px`) with `+added −removed`; a click unfolds the file and scrolls the diffs to it. `No changes.` for a repo with none.
- **Diffs**: one box per file, 20px apart, bordered `--line2`. Its header (`--raised`, the `▾`/`▸` fold mark, `<repo> <path>`, `new`, `deleted` or `renamed`, and `+added −removed`; a renamed file reads `<repo> <old path> → <path> renamed`) stays at the very top of the pane while its lines scroll, covering what's above it (square once stuck: the box's rounding is clipped). A click on it folds the file to just the header, or unfolds it; folding a file whose header is stuck scrolls back to the file's start. **A big change stays quick** (the CEO, 2026-11-02, after a review of a commit with a great many files was sluggish): a file with more than 500 changed lines (added and removed), or a lock file (`bun.lock`, `package-lock.json`, `yarn.lock`, `pnpm-lock.yaml`, `go.sum`, `Cargo.lock` and the like), starts folded, its header saying `large diff: open it to read` or `generated: open it to read`, as GitHub does; a folded file's lines aren't built at all, and an open file's lines are drawn by the browser only near the view (`content-visibility`), so a click redraws only its own file. Not virtualized: every open line stays in the page, so the browser's find still reaches it. **A moved file is one rename** (the CEO, 2026-11-04), never a deletion plus an addition: Foreman asks git for rename detection on every review diff, so its edits (if any) show as lines like any file's; with none, its box says `Moved, its content unchanged.` (any other file with no lines, such as an empty new file or a mode change, says `No lines changed.`). **The diff reads the same whatever the git config says** (the CEO's or the repo's, 2026-11-04): no external diff or textconv program configured there ever runs (it would run on the host), and colors, path prefixes, blank context lines, submodule lines and rename detection are fixed. A path git prints quoted (one with a quote, a backslash, a control or a non-ASCII character) shows as the path itself. Each line has its number on `main`'s side and on the branch's, then its text, colored added, removed or kept; hunk headers in `--tx4`.
- **Line comments**: hovering a line shows a `+` in its gutter, while the review is open. It opens a box under the line (`What should change here?`, `Cancel`, `Add to the draft`, primary: the CEO, 2026-11-03). A kept or added line is commented on the branch's side, a removed one on the target's: only against the target, since the other view's old side is the branch as it was. The line's comments sit under it, at the line they moved to.
- **General comments**, after the diffs: the general ones, and a box `A comment on the whole change…`. Then, listed apart with their `<repo> <path>:<line>` and the line's text: **Outdated** (their line changed since, or their file was deleted; a file the lead renamed since, with or without edits, keeps its comments, at their line in the new file: the CEO, 2026-11-04. The path stays as written, the new one is worked out each time, as the line is) and **Not in this view** (their repo was merged or dismissed, or the line isn't in the change shown).
- **A comment** shows `draft` (attention outline, `--attn`) with **Edit** and **Delete**, or `sent <time>`. A sent one can't change.
- **Decision bar** (at the foot, `--side`, while the review is open): when changes are requested, `Changes requested: waiting on <Lead> to fix them and ask again.`; the repos still open, in the room the buttons leave (the CEO, 2026-11-05: many repos pushed them off): a single one shows its line, cut, with no checkbox (the CEO, 2026-11-03); several show as one compact button, `▸ ☑ api · ☐ web · …  2/3` (each ticked or not, a conflicting one in `--err`, cut with an ellipsis), opening a popover above it with a checkbox per repo, all ticked at first, labeled with its link line (`<repo> · <branch> → <target> · checks <…>`, or `· conflicts with <target>` in `--err`), and, for a repo with more than one target, a select `Where <repo> merges` of its targets (the default first): a pick moves that repo of the review to that target (`PUT /api/reviews/{id}/target`), its diff is reckoned against it, a conflict found before is forgotten, comments on its removed lines become outdated (they were the old target's lines), and the lead is told; then **Dismiss** (danger), **Request changes (n)** (the draft count) and **Merge to <target>** (primary; `Merge` when the ticked repos' targets differ; disabled with none ticked, or until the ticked repos' diffs have loaded).
  - **With draft comments, Request changes leads** (the CEO, 2026-11-03): the order is **Dismiss**, **Merge to <target>** (default style) and **Request changes (n)** (primary, last). Merge then first asks `<n> draft comments won't be sent and will be lost. Merge anyway?` (`Cancel`, `Merge anyway`); merged in every repo, the drafts are deleted (a repo still open keeps them).
  - **Request changes** opens a note (`A note for <Lead>, with the n draft comments (optional)`) and `Send to <Lead>`, which needs a draft or a note. Sent, the page opens the lead's session, to watch it pick the changes up (the CEO, 2026-11-03).
  - **Dismiss** (the work isn't wanted) opens **What becomes of it**, radios, the first one picked. While the lead's deployment runs: `<Team> keeps working on <project>; the branches stay`, `Stop <Team> on <project>; the branches stay`, `Stop <Team> on <project> and drop the branches (kept aside, recoverable by hand)`. The branches go only with the team, as its agents work in them. Once it no longer runs: `Keep the branches`, `Drop the branches (kept aside, recoverable by hand)`. Only when the team keeps working is there a note (`A note for <Lead> (optional): <Lead> is told to stop work on it either way`); a note typed, then a choice that won't send it, stays in view with a warning in `--err`: `<Lead> won't get your note: the team stops.` (or `… the team isn't running.`). The button reads `Dismiss now`, or `Dismiss and send` with a note (the CEO, 2026-10-21). A branch that couldn't be dropped (a git error) shows in a toast, `Dismissed, but some branches stay`, a line each: `<branch> in <repo>: <why>.`
  - **Merge** merges each ticked repo; a toast says which merged. The toast opens the lead's session, to watch it pick the merge up. One that conflicts stays open, marked, and the toast names it with the reason.
  - **Only what the CEO read** (the CEO, 2026-10-21): Merge sends, for each ticked repo, the head its diff was shown at, and a repo whose branch moved since (the lead committed meanwhile) isn't merged: the toast says `<repo>: the branch has new commits since you loaded this page. Read them, then merge again`, and the page reloads its diff. The other repos merge as usual.
  - **Then back to the Inbox**: once Merge or Dismiss is done (Request changes opens the lead's session instead), the page goes back to the project's Inbox, whose next review is the next thing to do (the CEO, 2026-10-31).

## Decided

A repo of a review that is no longer open (merged, dismissed or withdrawn) keeps its diff (the CEO, 2026-11-01: a merged review's page used to be empty).

- **Recorded when it leaves open**: Foreman keeps the branch's head at that moment as `refs/foreman/decided/<merge request id>` (so git never collects it, and backup pruning leaves it alone) and the commit the branch parted from its target, on the merge request ([Merge request](../models/merge-request.md) `head`, `base`). For a branch the target took through a merge commit, the parting point is found from before that merge.
- **A target gone**: an open repo whose target's branch was deleted outside Foreman shows `<target> is gone.` in `--err` in the tree and, above the diffs, "<repo>: its target, <target>, is gone (its branch was deleted outside Foreman). Pick another target for it below to see its change and merge it." No diff, and Merge stays disabled while it is ticked.
- **Shown read-only**: the diff `base…head`, the repo's label in the tree followed by `· merged`, `· dismissed` or `· withdrawn`. No line comments, no decision bar for it.
- **Can't show it**: when nothing was recorded (decided before this, or a fast-forward merged on the forge, which leaves no parting point) or the commits are gone, the repo's group says `Can't show it.` and the page says why.

## What the lead gets

**Request changes** sends one message to the lead, typed into its session when idle:

```
From the CEO: changes requested on lead/ledger/atlas/invoices (backlog/invoices.md).

<the note>

- api internal/calc.go:5 `total := a + b`: Round it to cents.

- Split the migration out.

Fix them, commit(), then call request_merge again on the same branch.
```

The comments come in the order they were written: a line comment as `<repo> <path>:<line>` (its file and line now, a renamed file's new path; `<repo> <path>, removed line <n>` on the target's side; ` (changed since)` once outdated) with the line's text, a general one as its text. The review request resolves, and the review waits on the lead until its next `request_merge` on the branch, which raises it again with the new diff.

**Dismiss** means the work isn't wanted: it ends the review unmerged in every repo still open. While the team keeps working, the lead is told to stop, by Foreman, note or not: `From Foreman: the CEO dismissed the review of <branch> without merging (<repos>). Stop work on it, and don't request it again.`, then ` The CEO's note: <note>` when there is one. A team being stopped, or no longer running, is told nothing: there is nobody to read it. Its work items stay with the dismissed merge requests, so they leave In flight ([Pipeline and merges](./pipeline-and-merges.md#review-and-merge)).

## Withdrawn

A review can end before the CEO decides it, **withdrawn** (the CEO, 2026-10-22): its work went into another of the lead's branches, or the lead dropped it.

- **By the lead**, with `withdraw_merge(branch, reason, superseded_by?)`, on its own open review only, all of it ([Protocol → `withdraw_merge`](../architecture/protocol.md#withdraw_merge)). `superseded_by` names another of its branches with a review open that holds all of this one.
- **By Foreman**, when the CEO merges a review: every other open review of the same lead whose every open repo's target then holds all of it is withdrawn, superseded by the merged one, and its lead told: `From Foreman: the CEO merged <big>, which holds all of <small>, so Foreman withdrew the review of <small> (<id>, <repos>). Don't request it again.` Nothing is withdrawn when a lead requests a bigger branch: both stay open, and the CEO can merge the smaller one and dismiss the bigger (merging the smaller leaves the bigger open: it holds more).
- Either way the review leaves Needs you (its request resolves), shows **Withdrawn** under Done, and the CEO's **draft comments on it are deleted**: they can't be sent any more. A toast says so: `<Lead> withdrew <branch>` (or `Foreman withdrew …`) / `<reason>.`, then ` Your <n> draft comments on it are lost.` (in `--err`) when there were any. The branch stays. Its work items are freed (their commits are still on the lead branch): they go with the superseding review, or the lead's next request of a branch that holds them, unlike Dismiss, after which they stay with the dismissed merge requests.
